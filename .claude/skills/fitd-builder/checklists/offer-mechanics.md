# Offer Mechanics Checklist

**Purpose:** Gate a from-scratch offer definition (`/fitd-builder build`) before the
partner submits it to Extendly. Validates the OfferIQ 7-pillar mechanics only - no
narrative (that is `contextualize`). Every item is pass/fail; an item that can't pass is
recorded as an honest open gap in the packet's Verdict, never papered over.

## Inputs & fit
- [ ] Menu was resolved and used as exemplars (source stated); no offer was "selected"
- [ ] The deliverable/capability behind the offer is concrete and can be templated/automated
- [ ] Target market (niche + fits) is named

## Pillar 1 - the one problem
- [ ] One sentence, no "and/plus/as well as", tied to revenue (not a feeling/internal metric)
- [ ] Lands in the $100-300/mo wedge band (not a >$300 flagship, not a <$100 non-starter)
- [ ] Market anchor is **researched and cited** (competing tool/hire cost), not asserted

## Pillars 2-7 - each holds to its constraint
- [ ] Pillar 2 lead magnet: free, automated (no per-prospect labor), real personalized result, books the call, arms the owner with a call hook (does not auto-close)
- [ ] Pillar 3 intake: added intake under 5 min; no single question needs real thought; nothing to look up or decide; no duplication of standard intake
- [ ] Pillar 4 training: names one HighLevel surface; fits in 15 min; no re-teaching the standard walkthrough
- [ ] Pillar 5 implementation: human steps listed and totalled under 3 hours; templated, not a per-customer custom build; runnable from a written runbook
- [ ] Pillar 6 test & launch: shows the working result (test data ok), gets the explicit "turn it on," captures a concrete go-live plan
- [ ] Pillar 7 ascension: one specific next problem, surfaces during delivery, recorded for CS (not pitched at onboarding), points to a deliverable service

## Rigor (critical-coach)
- [ ] Weak pillar answers were challenged with the specific reason, not rubber-stamped
- [ ] Claims you were unsure about were researched or explicitly flagged as uncertain
- [ ] Each pillar carries a pass/fail with a reason; any fail is recorded in the Verdict

## House style & output
- [ ] No em dashes anywhere in the packet
- [ ] Output is markdown only (no YAML emitted)
- [ ] Written to the confirmed path, named `{offer-slug}.md`
